# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Keep reports focused on the project, and never publish another person's private data, credentials or original game files.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through https://ryanspice.com/ or GitHub. The maintainer may edit or remove content, lock threads or block accounts. Enforcement is at the maintainer's discretion; good-faith reports will be handled as privately as practical.

This is a small fan project, not an official LG, Microsoft or Chris Pirih space.
